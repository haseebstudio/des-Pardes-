Our Story

A Living Heritage
On Your Plate

Founded in 2010 by Toquir Ahmad — a Pakistani-American chef trained in the United States — Des Pardes was born from a vision to celebrate Pakistani culture through food and architecture. He chose Saidpur Village, a 500-year-old settlement preserved in the Margalla foothills, and transformed a weathered stone structure into the iconic fort-like dining space it is today.

Decorated with ethnic handicrafts, traditional baithaks, and an outdoor view of the hills, the restaurant has grown into one of Islamabad's most beloved dining destinations — equally adored by locals, tourists, and food explorers from across Pakistan.

What We Offer

More Than Just A Meal

Whether you're visiting for a quiet dinner, a family celebration, or a corporate lunch, Des Pardes has the setup — and the food — to make it memorable.

🍽️

Dine In

Settle into our traditional indoor seating with ethnic décor, folk touches, and the kind of warm hospitality you'd expect from a family kitchen — just a lot bigger.

🌿

Outdoor Seating

Our open-air terrace, perched at the edge of Saidpur Village with a view of the Margalla Hills, is arguably the best seat in Islamabad for a long, relaxed evening.

🎉

Private Events

Birthday dinners, family get-togethers, or small corporate lunches — we help you set the mood with the right food, the right space, and real attention to detail.

🥘

Hi-Tea Buffet

Enjoy our lavish hi-tea spread featuring live counters for chaat, gol gappay, jalebi, pancakes, BBQ bites, pasta, mini pizzas, assorted pastries, and much more.

📦

Takeaway

Can't stay long? Our takeaway service gets your favourite dishes packed up and ready — karahi, BBQ, biryani, and more — without the wait.

🚗

Valet Parking

We've got your parking sorted. Pull up, hand over the keys, and walk straight to your table. Full valet service available at the restaurant entrance.
